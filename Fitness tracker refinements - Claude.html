Getting Started

Hey — this is a fitness/nutrition tracker Josh built. It's still a work in progress, but it's ready for you to try out. Here's everything you need to know.

Opening it

It's a single file: generic_tracker_v1.html. Save it somewhere on your phone or laptop and open it with your browser (tap/double-click it, or open your browser and drag the file in). No app store, no install — it just runs.

Tip for your phone: once it's open in your browser, use the "Add to Home Screen" option (Safari: Share button → Add to Home Screen. Chrome: ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen). Then it opens full-screen like a normal app, no browser bar.

Setting it up (first time only)

It'll walk you through a few quick screens:

  1. Your name — so it can greet you properly.
  2. Save your data — connect Google Drive so your data is backed up and follows you between your phone and laptop. You can skip this and it'll just save on the one device instead — you can always connect it later from the ⚙️ Settings menu.
  3. About you — sex, age, height, current weight. This is what your nutrition targets are calculated from.
  4. Your goal — lose weight, maintain, or build muscle, plus how active you are day-to-day. Together with step 3, this sets your daily calorie and macro (protein/fat/carb) targets.
  5. Colour scheme — pick whichever one you like, change it anytime later.
  6. Review — shows your calculated targets and a plain-English explanation of how they were worked out. Hit "Start tracking" once you're happy.

You can change any of this later — see Settings below.

The tabs, quickly

  • Today — your daily snapshot: calories/protein/fat/carbs, water, meals logged so far, and your streak.
  • Log — add food. Three ways: snap a photo of your meal, type what you ate and let it estimate the macros, or type the numbers in yourself if you already know them.
  • Move — log steps and calories burned for the day (manual entry for now — no watch/app syncing yet).
  • Weight — log your weight and see your progress toward your goal.
  • Sleep — log total/deep/REM sleep if you want to track it.
  • Supps — a simple checklist for supplements you take.
  • Peptides — off by default. Turn it on in Settings if you want to track anything here.
  • Ask — a chat where you can ask nutrition questions, or just tell it what you ate/drank/how you slept and it'll log it for you.

Don't need one of these tabs? Turn it off in Settings (⚙️, top right) under "Visible Tabs" — Today, Log and Move always stay on, everything else is optional.

The streak

Hit your calorie and protein targets for the day and it counts toward your streak. Open the app the next day and you'll get a little "welcome back" message telling you how many days running you've kept it up. Missing a day resets the streak, but your best-ever streak is always shown too, so one off day doesn't erase your progress.

Settings (⚙️ top right)

Everything from setup is editable here — your name, stats, goal, activity level, and your macro targets (with the explanation of why they're set the way they are). You can also switch colour schemes and turn tabs on/off from here.

A few things worth knowing

  • This is an early build. Things might look a bit rough in places or change as Josh keeps working on it — let him know if anything breaks or feels off.
  • The AI features (photo/text lookup, chat) run through Josh's own account. That's fine for now since it's just us using it, but it means there's no separate login for you — just open the app and go.
  • Your data lives on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you connect it during setup (or later in Settings). Nobody else can see it.
  • No wearable syncing yet (Garmin, Apple Watch, etc.) — steps and sleep are manual entry for now.

If anything's confusing or you want something changed, just tell Josh — this one's easy to tweak.
